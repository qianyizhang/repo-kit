# Repository documentation

- **Outcome** · Lead with what the reader can accomplish or decide. State what observable result counts as success and name any material limits.
- **Ownership** · Keep each contract with its owner. Link directly to the relevant section, schema, command, or test. Document intent, invariants, decisions, and exceptions that the executable source cannot explain.
- **Structure** · Make entry points short routers. Use a table for choices and their outcomes, a list for independent checks, and an ordered walkthrough when sequence matters. Give setup, migration, and recovery the commands, prerequisites, and checkpoints needed to complete them.
- **Agent guidance** · State the completion condition, local constraints, and when to consult a deeper reference. Leave routine execution choices to the agent. Name decisions that require authorization.
- **Review** · Follow the entry point to the intended result. Check examples, links, and acceptance claims against current behavior. Keep planned behavior and historical results explicitly dated or scoped. Consolidate duplicate guidance after preserving unique rationale and repairing its readers.
